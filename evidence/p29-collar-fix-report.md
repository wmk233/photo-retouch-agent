## 1. The ~799 px old white collar (mask fix)

The collar split starts at CHIN + collar_margin_ratio * face_w, so a garment
that rises to the jaw keeps a margin-height strip frozen with the face. Measured
on the reference (collar_band_profile.json), that band is
protected & below-chin & parsed-face & ~skin = **805 px**. Its tone is mostly
the shadowed throat, not cloth: median luma 53.8, only 135 px above 1.0x the
subject's own skin luminance (band_luma_ratio_hist).

### What changed

New knob on SemanticMaskConfig (backend/app/domains/portrait/edit_mask.py),
mirroring the existing collar_split pattern:

- collar_margin_recovery_enabled = True
- collar_margin_recovery_ring_ratio = 0.02  (a 5.1 px ring at this face width)
- collar_margin_recovery_luma_ratio = 0.90
- collar_margin_recovery_chroma = 45.0

A protected pixel is released only when **all** hold: below the chin row; not
the subject's skin; not darker than 0.35x the skin luminance (the shadow under
the jaw); and carrying independent garment evidence -- the parser's own
Upper-clothes label, or a bright desaturated highlight on the collar's rim
(luma > 0.90x ref, chroma < 45). The identity guard, the spectacle band and the
parsed hair are subtracted back out and the count that had to be dropped is
reported (collarMarginRecoveryRefusedByGuardPixels).

The largest blob is **identical in pixel count and bounding box** in both
renders. The hair pass neither created nor removed it, so its source is settled:
**the pale content is generated by the Clothing Pass**
(ghost_source_compare.json, identical_pixels_and_bbox: true). The mean colour
where the original knit survived is a pale desaturated sage/ivory

hair/clothing overlap. Neither was done -- the hair/clothing overlap stays at
13,357 px.
